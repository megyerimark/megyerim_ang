Quote requests and the project quiz

Newsletter (offers and discounts)

Data: email address, chosen language, where you subscribed, and proof of consent (the consent text, time, IP address, browser identifier).
Purpose: informing you about offers, discounts and news.
Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); Section 6 of Hungarian Act XLVIII of 2008 on commercial advertising). The subscription only becomes valid after you click the link in the confirmation email (double opt-in).
Withdrawal: at any time, without giving reasons, via the unsubscribe link in every email or by email to us; this does not affect the lawfulness of earlier mailings.
Retention: until you unsubscribe; unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted automatically after 30 days. After unsubscribing, we keep the address only to prove that we no longer send emails to it.

Private project page, messages and documents

Each quote request has a personal, random access link sent to you by email. It shows the status, content and amount of the proposal and the project milestones; you can send messages and download documents uploaded for you there. Messages (content, author, time) and documents are kept in private storage, and the time of accepting the proposal is recorded. Please do not share the link; it expires and can be replaced on request (the old one then stops working). The access key is stored only encrypted or in a non-reversible (hashed) form.
Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(b). Retention: as for quote requests.

Contract, invoicing and payment

When a proposal is accepted, we record the time of acceptance, a fingerprint of the accepted proposal, the version of the Terms and Conditions, and the IP address and browser identifier used, to prove the conclusion of the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Invoice data must be kept for 8 years under Section 169 of Hungarian Act C of 2000 on accounting (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

Card payments (deposit, maintenance subscription) take place on the secure payment page of Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. (Ireland); we never see or store card details. We pass your name, email address and the amount to Stripe. Stripe also acts as an independent controller for fraud prevention and its legal obligations (stripe.com/privacy).

Visitor statistics

We use anonymous, aggregated event counting (e.g. page views, button clicks, traffic source). We do not store IP addresses, browser identifiers or cookies for this, so the data cannot be linked to a person. If “Do Not Track” is enabled in your browser, we do not count. The traffic source is kept in the browser's session storage until the tab is closed. Anonymous events are deleted after 13 months.

Cookies and local storage

We only use strictly necessary cookies: when a form is submitted, a security (CSRF protection) cookie and a session cookie that prevent abuse and expire at the end of the browsing session or after 2 hours at most. Your chosen language, your cookie-banner choice and display settings are kept in your browser's local storage. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Google reviews (external content): if Google reviews are shown on the home page, they are loaded by Trustindex, which receives the visitor's IP address and may set cookies. We load this content only after your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time with the “Cookie settings” button in the footer. See the Trustindex privacy policy at trustindex.io.

Security logging

To prevent abuse and fix errors, the server may keep technical logs for a short period (e.g. IP address, time, requested address). Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating the service securely (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Retention: according to the hosting provider's logging policy, typically up to 30 days.
